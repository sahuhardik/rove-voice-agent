## Troubleshooting articles

### Blurry video / out of focus video

**Causes:** Something in front of the lens: protective film still attached, dirty lens, dirty windshield, low-light conditions, or lower video resolution setting [help: blurry-video]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Remove the protective lens film** — check if a clear protective plastic film is still attached over the lens [help: blurry-video]
2. **Clean the dash cam lens** — gently wipe using a soft, clean microfiber cloth [help: blurry-video]
3. **Clean the windshield inside and outside** — clean the glass inside and outside, especially the area directly in front of the camera lens [help: blurry-video]
4. **Understand low-light video limits** — night driving, very dark scenes, rain, glare, or strong headlights may make video appear grainy or less sharp; this can be normal [help: blurry-video]
5. **Check the video resolution setting** — go to the video settings and select a higher resolution, such as 4K or 2.5K, if your model supports it [help: blurry-video]

**After each step:** Record a short test video in daylight and review the original video file [help: blurry-video]

**If fix fails:** Contact ROVE support team [help: blurry-video]

### Camera does not turn on (completely dead)

**Causes:** Cable issue, charger issue, memory card issue, or camera itself [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]

**Before you begin:** Bring the camera indoors. Use your iPhone or Android smartphone's USB charger along with the short USB cable included in the ROVE box. Plug everything into a standard 110V wall outlet — not your car — to eliminate vehicle power as a variable [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Test with a different USB cable** — swap to the 12FT long L-shaped power cable or the short 1M USB cable (both included in your ROVE box). Connect to your smartphone's USB charger and plug into a wall outlet [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]
2. **Remove the memory card** — eject the microSD card from the camera. Unplug the power cable, then plug it back in. Check if the camera powers on without the memory card inserted [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]
3. **Press the Power button once** — with the power cable plugged in, press the Power button once. On some ROVE models, the camera does not automatically power on when connected — a single press of the power button is required [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]
4. **Perform a hard reset** —
   - Locate the Reset button at the bottom of the dash cam
   - Use a straightened paper clip or a pen tip to press and hold the reset button once while the power cord is plugged in
   - If the camera still does not turn on, unplug the power cable first, then repeat the reset process [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]

**If fix fails:** Contact ROVE support. They will promptly replace any defective item [help: camera-does-not-turn-on]

### Camera does not turn off (always stays on)

**Causes:** Vehicle's 12V cigarette lighter socket stays "live" even after the engine is switched off — this is called an "always hot" power source. This is a vehicle wiring characteristic, not a defect in the dash cam [help: camera-does-not-turn-off]

**Note:** ROVE dash cams are designed to automatically power on when they receive power and shut down automatically when power is removed [help: camera-does-not-turn-off]

### Cannot read license plate number clearly

**Causes:** Lighting, distance, speed, angle, windshield glare, video resolution, and how the video is played back. Plates are harder to read when the vehicle is far away, at a sharp angle, moving quickly, or affected by glare, headlights, shadows, rain, or dirty glass [help: licence-plate]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Understand real-world capture limits** — plates are easier to read when the vehicle is close, centered, and moving at a similar speed [help: licence-plate]
2. **Clean the windshield and lens** — clean the windshield area in front of the lens and gently clean the dash cam lens with a soft microfiber cloth [help: licence-plate]
3. **Play the original file using VLC Media Player** — do not judge video quality from a compressed copy, social media upload, text message, or phone preview. Transfer the original video file to your computer and play it using VLC Media Player [help: licence-plate]
4. **Try the ROVE GPS Player** — use the ROVE GPS Player from the website to view videos with Google Maps, G-sensor data, date, direction, and speed during playback [help: licence-plate]

**If fix fails:** Contact ROVE support team [help: licence-plate]

### Dash cam screen frozen

**Causes:** Temporary software stuck, power issue, or memory card issue [help: frozen-screen]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Unplug and reconnect the power cable** — remove the power cable from the dash cam, wait a few seconds, then plug it back in. Turn the dash cam ON and check if the screen still freezes [help: frozen-screen]
2. **Press the RESET button once, if your model has one** — if the screen is still frozen and no buttons respond, press the RESET button at the bottom of the camera once using a paper clip or the tip of a pen. This will restart the camera [help: frozen-screen]
3. **Remove the memory card and test again** — power OFF the dash cam, remove the memory card, then power the dash cam back ON. Check whether the camera still freezes without the memory card inserted [help: frozen-screen]
4. **Try a spare compatible memory card** — if the dash cam works normally without the original memory card, try a spare compatible microSD card to confirm whether the original card is causing the freeze [help: frozen-screen]

**If fix fails:** Contact ROVE support team [help: frozen-screen]

### Dash cam repeatedly turning ON and OFF / random restarting

**Causes:** Insufficient or unstable power from the USB car charger, USB power cable, vehicle power source, memory card, or in rare cases the dash cam itself [help: repeatedly-on-off] [help: random-restarting]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Reset the camera** — use a small object such as a paperclip or the tip of a pen to press the RESET button on the camera. This clears temporary glitches and returns the camera to factory settings [help: repeatedly-on-off]
   - **Note:** Does not apply to the ROVE R3 dashcam, as it does not have a reset button [help: random-restarting]
2. **Test the dash cam with a reliable wall power source** — bring the dash cam indoors and plug it into a standard 110V wall outlet using your phone's USB charger [help: repeatedly-on-off]
3. **Test with both ROVE USB cables** — while testing with the wall outlet, try both the long and short USB cables provided by ROVE to identify whether one cable is loose, damaged, or not providing stable power [help: repeatedly-on-off]
4. **Remove the memory card and retest** — turn OFF the camera, remove the memory card, then repeat the wall outlet test using both ROVE USB cables. A faulty or corrupted memory card can cause camera instability [help: repeatedly-on-off]

**If fix fails:** Contact ROVE support. They will assist with determining whether a replacement part or additional troubleshooting is needed [help: repeatedly-on-off]

### Memory card too slow error

**Causes:** Memory card can no longer maintain the required write speed for continuous video recording. Memory cards have a limited lifespan; over time, repeated recording and overwriting may cause the card to slow down, develop bad sectors, or become corrupted [help: memory-card-too-slow]

**Before you begin:** This warning means your dash cam is functioning properly and is alerting you that the memory card may need maintenance or replacement [help: memory-card-too-slow]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Format the Memory Card** — try formatting the memory card using the dash cam settings menu or a computer [help: memory-card-too-slow]
2. **Replace the Memory Card** — replace with a Class 10 U3 high-speed microSD card recommended for dash cam use [help: memory-card-too-slow]
3. **Upgrade to a High-Speed ROVE Memory Card** — ROVE memory cards are specifically designed for continuous dash cam recording and high-performance video writing speeds [help: memory-card-too-slow]

**Important notes:**
- Always safely eject the memory card when using a computer [help: memory-card-too-slow]
- Regularly format the memory card inside the dash cam for best performance [help: memory-card-too-slow]
- Low-quality or counterfeit cards may cause recording issues [help: memory-card-too-slow]
- Replace memory cards periodically depending on recording usage [help: memory-card-too-slow]

**If fix fails:** Contact ROVE support team [help: memory-card-too-slow]

### Overheating

**Causes:**
1. Extended exposure to direct sunlight — cabin temperatures can exceed 140°F (60°C), causing internal temperature to rise above operating range [help: overheating]
2. Incompatible or faulty microSD card — non-U3-rated card forces processor to work harder, generating additional heat [help: overheating]
3. Using unsupported power sources — power banks, OBD2 adapters, third-party USB chargers may cause unstable current and heat buildup [help: overheating]
4. Parking Mode running in extreme heat — without air circulation or A/C, interior temperatures spike quickly [help: overheating]

**Operating temperature range:** -4°F to 140°F (-20°C to 60°C). Beyond this, the device may slow down, shut off, or stop recording [help: overheating]

**Checks and fixes (in order):**

1. **Use a High-Quality, U3-Class MicroSD Card** — use ROVE PRO, ROVE Ultimate, or ROVE Industrial U3 microSD card [help: overheating]
2. **Format the Memory Card Regularly** — format your SD card in-camera once every 1-2 weeks [help: overheating]
3. **Avoid Direct Sunlight** — park in shade or use a windshield sunshade; mount away from direct sunlight if your vehicle allows [help: overheating]
4. **Use Only ROVE-Approved Power Sources** — use original ROVE USB-C car charger or ROVE Ultimate 3-Lead Hardwire Kit (regulated 5V/3.4A output) [help: overheating]
5. **Adjust Parking Mode Settings** — set shorter Parking Mode timer (1-3 hours) or disable during hotter days [help: overheating]
6. **Keep Firmware Up to Date** — contact support team for latest firmware and installation instructions [help: overheating]

**If issue persists:** If dash cam consistently overheats, shuts off without warning, or displays "High Temperature" error, stop using the device and contact ROVE right away. If under warranty, they will work on repair or replacement options [help: overheating]

### WiFi: Phone joins camera Wi-Fi then drops / "no internet"

**Why this happens:** The phone tries to connect to the internet through the dash cam's WiFi, but the dash cam does not provide internet access, so the phone disconnects and reconnects to cellular network repeatedly. This is how Apple designed its operating system to work [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]

**Quick fixes:**

- **iPhone:** You may see "No Internet Connection" or "does not appear to be connected to the Internet". Tap "Keep Trying Wi-Fi". Don't let it disconnect, or you'll have to forget the ROVE network and join again. A blue check mark next to the ROVE network means you're connected [official: connect-rove-to-phone]

- **Android:** You'll see "No Internet". Wait 10 to 15 seconds for the pop-up, then tap "Always connect", or tick "Don't ask again for this network" and tap YES [official: connect-rove-to-phone]

**To prevent repeated voice guidance ("WiFi Is Connected - WiFi Is Disconnected"):**
- Turn OFF the WiFi when you don't need it. You only need WiFi to view or download recorded videos using the ROVE APP [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]
- For ROVE R2-4K and ROVE R2-4K PRO: press & hold the emergency lock button for two seconds [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]
- For ROVE R3: tap the home screen then tap the blue WiFi icon [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]

**Firmware update to disable voice guidance only:**
- Check camera's system settings for option to Turn ON or OFF the WiFi Connection Voice Guidance. If present, turn it OFF [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]
- If option not present, update to latest firmware from Rove's firmware page [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]

**If prefer to prevent auto-connect:** Turn off "Auto Join" or "Auto Re-Connect" feature from your Smartphone's settings to prevent automatic connection to dash cam's Wi-Fi. To connect manually: go to Settings > Wi-Fi and select JOIN [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]

## Firmware and app guidance

### How to update firmware

**Option 1: OTA (Over The Air) via ROVE App**
1. Ensure ROVE Dash Cam is powered on and within range
2. Open ROVE App on mobile device
3. Connect app with dash cam
4. Navigate to App Menu (usually in main menu)
5. Select 'Firmware Update' option
6. App automatically checks for available firmware updates
7. If new version available, notification displays: "There is a NEW Firmware Update Available!"
8. Click 'UPDATE Firmware'
9. Pop-up indicates firmware update started successfully
10. Camera screen displays progress — allow to complete without interruption
11. Camera automatically restarts and boots up with new firmware [official: firmware-update-downloads]

**Important for OTA:** Stay connected to dash cam's WiFi and don't disconnect to use other Wi-Fi networks that provide internet [help: wifi-connected-disconnected]

**Option 2: Manual Firmware Update (requires computer)**

Before starting:
- **DESTRUCTIVE:** Format memory card first by going into camera's system settings. Backup videos before formatting if needed [official: firmware-update-downloads]

Steps:
1. Download latest firmware for your Rove Dash Camera from Rove's firmware page [official: firmware-update-downloads]
2. Insert memory card into PC/MAC directly if you have memory card slot, or connect camera via USB and select "Connect as USB Flash Drive" [official: firmware-update-downloads]
3. Copy firmware files directly to memory card — **do NOT create any new folder** [official: update-firmware-r2-4k]
4. **Important:** Ensure firmware's file name doesn't get changed, otherwise camera will NOT recognize the firmware update [official: update-firmware-r2-4k]
5. Keep camera plugged into power & turned OFF [official: firmware-update-downloads]
6. Insert memory card with firmware files into camera [official: firmware-update-downloads]
7. Press POWER BUTTON once to start update [official: firmware-update-downloads]
8. Look for flashing RED/BLUE LED on left upper corner indicating firmware is being updated [official: firmware-update-downloads]
9. **Critical:** Make sure power doesn't get disconnected during firmware update [official: firmware-update-downloads]
10. Dash Cam will reboot automatically after applying upgrade [official: firmware-update-downloads]

**After any firmware update:** **DESTRUCTIVE:** Format memory card immediately after camera turns on, otherwise camera will keep updating firmware every time it restarts [official: firmware-update-downloads]

**Check firmware version:** Go to camera's system settings > firmware version [official: update-firmware-r2-4k]

### How to connect the app

See detailed steps in "App says no device found" section above. Summary:

1. Install ROVE Dash Cam app from App Store or Google Play [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
2. Create free account first while phone is on normal internet — NOT connected to dash cam WiFi yet [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
3. Allow location access when app asks [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
4. Ensure memory card is in camera and camera is powered on [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
5. Turn on camera's WiFi (method varies by model — see "How to turn on WiFi on each ROVE model" below) [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
6. Open ROVE app and tap "Connect to ROVE Dash Cam WiFi" or go to phone's Settings > WiFi [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
7. Join camera's network (starts with "ROVE" and model name) [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
8. Enter password: 12345678 (also shown on camera screen) [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
9. Handle "No Internet" message appropriately for iPhone or Android (see above) [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
10. Return to ROVE app — select camera if previously added, or tap Add New Device [official: connect-rove-to-phone]

**How to turn on WiFi on each ROVE model:**
- **ROVE R2-4K:** Press and hold Emergency Lock button for 2 seconds, or System Settings > WiFi [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
- **ROVE R2-4K DUAL:** Press and hold Emergency Lock button for 2 seconds, or System Settings > WiFi [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
- **ROVE R2-4K DUAL PRO:** Press and hold Emergency Lock button for 2 seconds, or System Settings > WiFi [official: connect-rove-to-phone]
- **ROVE R3:** Tap screen > Settings > System Settings > tap WiFi icon (solid red light means WiFi is on) [official: connect-rove-to-phone]

## Safety and data-loss warnings

- **DESTRUCTIVE:** Always back up important video files before formatting memory card [official: firmware-update-downloads] [help: r3-no-gps-live-speed] [help: wifi-not-connecting]
- **DESTRUCTIVE:** Formatting memory card wipes all files from the card and sets it up afresh [help: know-your-dashcam-how-to]
- **DESTRUCTIVE:** Setting camera to DEFAULT SETTINGS erases settings — back up footage first [help: r3-no-gps-live-speed]
- **DESTRUCTIVE:** After firmware update, format memory card immediately or camera will keep updating firmware every restart [official: firmware-update-downloads]
- **Critical during firmware update:** Make sure power doesn't get disconnected while firmware is being updated [official: firmware-update-downloads] [official: update-firmware-r2-4k]
- **Camera protection:** ROVE dash cams are not waterproof — do not expose to rain, moisture, or water [help: know-your-dashcam-how-to]
- **Temperature limits:** Designed to operate between -4°F and 140°F (-20°C to 60°C). Beyond this, device may slow down, shut off, or stop recording [help: overheating]
