# ROVE R2-4K PRO — knowledge pack

## How to recognise this model

- **Wi-Fi network name pattern:** "ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_*******" [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Labels:** Model name "R2-4K PRO" appears on packaging and manual; FCC ID: 2A3IW-R24KPRO [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Screen:** 2.4" IPS display [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Power connector:** USB Type-C [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Physical buttons (NOT touch screen):** MENU/MODE button, UP button, DOWN button, OK/CONFIRM button, EMERGENCY LOCK button, POWER button [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Lens:** Single front camera, 150° ultra-wide angle, can rotate 360° [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **LED indicator:** Red LED on top [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Difference from other Rove models:** Uses Supercapacitor (not internal battery), unlike R2-4K which has small Li-ion battery [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

## Key facts

- **Power and supercapacitor:** Uses Supercapacitor (NOT an internal battery). Holds charge for about 2 seconds to save last video file safely before shutting off. Must be plugged into power at all times for continuous recording. [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Memory card type:** Class 10, U3 speed Micro-SD card required [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Memory card capacity:** Up to 512GB max supported [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Memory card format:** Must format in camera before first use to prevent errors [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Video resolutions:** 4K 3840x2160 @30fps; 2K 2560x1440 @30fps & 60fps; 1080P 1920x1080 @60fps; 1080P 1920x1080 @30fps [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Wi-Fi band:** Dual-band 2.4GHz and 5.0GHz [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **GPS:** Built-in GPS for geo-tagging [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Parking mode requirements:** ROVE Ultimate USB Type-C Hardwire Kit (NOT included) required; connects to car's fuse box (NOT directly to battery terminals) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Operating temperature:** -15°C (5°F) to +60°C (+140°F) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **LED/indicator meanings:** Blinking RED = Recording video; Solid RED = Not recording video; NO RED LED = Camera turned OFF [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

## How-to procedures

### Power on/off
**Auto power ON/OFF (default behavior):**
- Camera turns ON and starts recording automatically when power connected (when car engine started) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Camera stops recording, saves last video file, and turns OFF automatically within 1-2 seconds when power disconnected (when car key turned to LOCK position) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Manual power ON:** Press the Power Button once [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Manual power OFF:** Press and hold Power Button for 2-3 seconds [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Format the card
**DESTRUCTIVE** — deletes all data on memory card
1. Press OK button to STOP video recording first or clear any error message [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Press MENU button once to enter video settings [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. Press MENU button again to switch to System Settings [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Press DOWN button to scroll down until you see FORMAT [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
5. Press OK button to enter format submenu [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
6. Press DOWN button to highlight FORMAT [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
7. Press OK button to confirm format [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
8. Press MENU button to exit settings [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Reset to defaults
**DESTRUCTIVE** — resets all camera settings to factory defaults; does NOT delete video files
1. Go to System Settings [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Scroll down until you see DEFAULT SETTING [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. Select OK to confirm [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Hardware reset (if camera frozen):**
Press RESET button at bottom of camera ONCE with paper clip or tip of pen while camera is turned ON [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Set date/time and time zone
1. Go to System Settings > Date/Time [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Select date and time manually, OR [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. Set GMT/UTC time-zone value so camera sets date/time automatically when GPS signal locks [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Time zone values:** GMT -12:00 to GMT +12:00 [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- USA examples: Hawaii GMT (-10:00), Alaska GMT (-9:00), Pacific GMT (-8:00), Mountain GMT (-7:00), Central GMT (-6:00), Eastern GMT (-5:00) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Daylight savings: Use one hour less when DST active (March-Nov), one hour more when DST ends (Nov-March) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Connect to phone app over Wi-Fi
1. Download "ROVE Dash Cam" app from App Store or Google Play Store [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. First-time users: Register and create free account using internet (home WiFi or cellular) — required only once [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. **Turn ON WiFi on camera:** Long press EMERGENCY LOCK BUTTON for 2 seconds, OR go to camera's SYSTEM SETTINGS to turn ON WiFi [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Open ROVE app, click "Connect to ROVE Dash Cam WiFi" button [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
5. Look for WiFi network "ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_*******" in phone's WiFi settings [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
6. Join using default password: **12345678** (displayed on camera screen) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
7. Return to ROVE APP, select R2-4K PRO model, click proceed [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Android extra step:** When "No Internet" message appears, wait 10-15 seconds for popup, select "Don't ask again" then "YES" to stay connected [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**iPhone:** "No Internet Connection" message is normal — ignore it. Look for blue checkmark indicating connection, then open ROVE app [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Download/playback footage

**On camera directly:**
1. Press and hold MENU button for 2 seconds to switch to Photo mode [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Press and hold MENU button again to switch to Playback mode [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. Use UP/DOWN buttons to scroll through videos [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Press OK to play video [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
5. Press -- to go back to video list [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**On PC/Mac via USB:**
1. Connect camera using included short USB Type-C data cable [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. When message pops up, select "Connect as USB Flash Drive" [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. Find added memory card drive on computer [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Double-click R2-4K PRO folder for unlocked videos, or "EVENT" folder for locked videos [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**On PC/Mac via SD card adapter:**
1. Remove memory card from camera (camera must be OFF) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Insert into PC/Mac memory card slot or SD card adapter [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**On smartphone via WiFi:**
- Use ROVE app after connecting via WiFi (see Wi-Fi procedure above) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Recommended video players:** VLC Media Player (free at www.videolan.org) — NOT Windows Media Player or QuickTime Player [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Lock/protect a file

**While recording video:**
- Press EMERGENCY LOCK button once — locks current loop cycle video file so it won't be deleted by loop cycle [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**In Playback mode:**
1. Go to Playback Settings > Protect [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Options: Lock Current, Unlock Current, Lock All, Unlock All [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Note:** Locked videos saved in "EVENT" folder. Even locked videos can be deleted if event video allocation (30%) is full — oldest locked video deleted first [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Parking mode
**Prerequisite:** ROVE Ultimate USB Type-C Hardwire Kit required (NOT included), connected to car's fuse box [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Settings available:**
- OFF [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Time-Lapse 1fps & G-Sensor [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Motion Detection & G-Sensor [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Collision Detection Only [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**How Time-Lapse + G-Sensor works:**
1. When car turned OFF, camera stops continuous recording, enters timelapse 1fps mode automatically [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. If impact reaches G-Sensor level: camera stops timelapse, records 1-min continuous video, saves & locks to EVENT folder, returns to timelapse [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. When car started next time: camera exits parking mode, enters normal recording [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Voice alert "Parking Mode got activated while you were away" plays when car started if parking mode was triggered [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**How Motion Detection + G-Sensor works:**
1. When car turned OFF, camera stops continuous recording, enters motion detection mode (camera stays ON idle, LCD off after seconds) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. Records only when motion detected in front of camera [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. If impact reaches G-Sensor level: stops motion detection, records 1-min continuous video, saves & locks to EVENT folder, returns to motion detection [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Voice alert plays when car started if triggered [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**How Collision Detection Only works:**
1. When car turned OFF, camera turns OFF but actively senses impact [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
2. If impact reaches Parking Mode G-Sensor level: camera turns ON, records 1-min video, locks & saves to EVENT folder, turns OFF [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
3. Voice alert and on-screen popup when car started if triggered [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
4. Press OK to acknowledge popup, then check locked videos [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Parking Mode G-Sensor levels:** 1-9, where 1 = lowest (smallest) impact detection, 9 = higher (larger) impact detection [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Motion detection
- Part of Parking Mode settings (see Parking Mode procedure) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- When enabled, camera records only when motion detected in front of camera field of view [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### G-sensor
**While video recording (driving):**
- Automatically triggers during impact to lock current loop cycle video file [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Manual lock: Press EMERGENCY LOCK button once while recording [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Parking Mode G-Sensor:** Adjustable 1-9 sensitivity for parking mode impact detection [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Loop recording
**Settings:** OFF, 1-MIN (default), 3-MIN, 5-MIN, 10-MIN [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**How it works:**
- When ON: camera continuously records, deletes oldest video files automatically when memory card full [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Splits each video file to selected length [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- When OFF: records each file 4GB in size until card full, then shows "Card Full" message and stops recording [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

**Memory allocation:** Normal videos 60%, Event (locked) videos 30% — when total reaches >90%, loop recording begins [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- If event files <30%, normal videos can use unused event space until 90% total [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- If event files reach 30% max, oldest locked video deleted to make room for new event [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

## Troubleshooting playbook

### Wi-Fi: phone joins camera Wi-Fi then drops / "no internet"
- **Likely causes:** (1) Normal behavior — camera WiFi does not provide internet, (2) Android phone auto-disconnecting due to "No Internet" detection [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Questions/checks:** Is phone Android or iPhone? Did "No Internet" popup appear? [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Fixes:**
  - **Android:** Wait 10-15 seconds after connecting for popup, select "Don't ask again" then "YES" to stay connected [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  - **iPhone:** "No Internet Connection" message is normal — ignore it, proceed to open ROVE app when blue checkmark appears [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Escalate:** If customer still cannot maintain connection after proper steps — escalate [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Wi-Fi: camera Wi-Fi not in the list
- **Likely causes:** (1) WiFi not turned on in camera, (2) phone too far from camera (range ~10M), (3) camera powered off [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Questions/checks:** Is camera powered on? Is red LED showing (solid or blinking)? Is camera within 10 meters? [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Fixes:**
  1. Ensure camera is powered on — press Power Button once if needed [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  2. Turn on WiFi on camera: Long press EMERGENCY LOCK button 2 seconds, or go to System Settings > WiFi [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  3. Move phone closer to camera (within 10M, no obstructions) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Escalate:** If WiFi still not broadcasting after verified power and WiFi enabled — escalate [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Wi-Fi: password rejected
- **Likely causes:** (1) Wrong password entered, (2) viewing wrong camera screen for password [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Questions/checks:** What password is customer entering? Is camera screen showing the password? [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Fixes:** Default password is **12345678** — verify this exact password is displayed on camera screen and entered correctly [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Escalate:** If default password displayed but still rejected — escalate [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Wi-Fi: connection drops repeatedly
- **Likely causes:** (1) Phone too far from camera, (2) obstructions blocking signal, (3) interference from other devices [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Questions/checks:** Distance from camera? Any obstructions? Same behavior on different phones? [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Fixes:**
  1. Move within 10M range, no obstructions [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  2. Try switching WiFi band: System Settings > WiFi Mode — try 5.0GHz for faster speed/shorter range, or 2.4GHz for longer range/slower speed [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  3. Reset camera: press RESET button once with paper clip [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Escalate:** If drops persist within 3M clear line of sight — escalate [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Wi-Fi: playback/download very slow
- **Likely causes:** (1) Using 2.4GHz band instead of 5.0GHz, (2) large 4K file sizes, (3) phone/app compatibility [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Questions/checks:** Which WiFi band is selected? What video resolution? [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Fixes:** Change to 5.0GHz WiFi mode: System Settings > WiFi Mode > 5.0 GHz [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  - Note: 5.0GHz provides faster speed but less range; 2.4GHz provides longer range but slower speed [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Escalate:** If still slow on 5.0GHz with good signal — escalate [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Firmware: update stalled
- **Likely causes:** (1) WiFi connection unstable during update, (2) app issue [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Questions/checks:** Is camera still connected to app? What stage did it stall at? [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Fixes:**
  1. Ensure stable WiFi connection — close to camera, proper band selected [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  2. Restart update process in app [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
  3. Contact customer care for assistance [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Escalate:** If multiple attempts fail — escalate to customer care (explicitly stated in manual) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

## Safety and data-loss warnings

### Heat warnings
- **Operating temperature:** -15°C (5°F) to +60°C (+140°F); do not expose to temperatures above 140°F (60°C) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- **Storage temperature:** -25°C (-13°F) to +75°C (+167°F) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Camera automatically shuts down when temperature exceeded to prevent damage [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not leave in direct sunlight for long periods where temperature exceeds 140°F (60°C) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Remove camera when not in use to prevent heat exposure and theft [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Supercapacitor more heat resistant than Li-ion batteries [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Power safety
- Use only supplied ROVE charger to power R2-4K PRO [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Charger: 5V, 1.5 AMP (max 3.1 AMP) — do NOT use heavy-duty charger over 3.1 AMP [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not use charger if cord broken or damaged [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not modify wires or connect dash cam directly to 12/24V battery — requires step-down circuit (Hardwire Kit) [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Mounting safety
- Do not install where it blocks driver's view of road, mirrors, or airbag deployment [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not place camera loose on dashboard or in front of airbags [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not mount where it can be distraction if loose and falls while driving [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Avoid mounting on dot-matrix patterned windshield area — suction will fail [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- If vehicle has side curtain airbags, do not run cables past airbags — prevents deployment [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Some states/countries prohibit mounting on windshield or certain areas — user's responsibility to comply [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]

### Operating safety
- Do not operate or install camera while driving — pull off road first [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not expose to rain, moisture, water — not damp-proof [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not use cleaning solvents — use soft moist cloth only [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Keep away from children — not a toy [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- For indoor/vehicle use only [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
- Do not drop, puncture, or disassemble [manual: ROVE_R2-4K_PRO_User_Manual_ManualsLib-mirror]
