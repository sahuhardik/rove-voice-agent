## Key facts

- **Power and supercapacitor:** Uses supercapacitor (NOT battery). Holds charge for about 2 seconds to save last video file safely when power disconnects [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]. Must be plugged into power at all times for continuous operation [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]. Camera turns ON automatically when power received, turns OFF automatically 1-2 seconds after power disconnects [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **Memory card requirements:** Class-10, U3 or higher speed microSD card. Maximum 1TB supported [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]. Recommended: ROVE PRO (everyday affordable), ROVE Ultimate (premium choice), or ROVE Industrial (high endurance) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]. Included card: 128GB ROVE PRO [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **Video resolutions - Front camera:** 4K 3840x2160P @30fps, 2.7K 2592x1520P @30fps, 2K 2560x1440P @60fps (HDR not supported), 2K 2560x1440P @30fps, FHD 1920x1080P @60fps (HDR not supported), FHD 1920x1080P @30fps [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **Video resolutions - Rear camera:** 2K 2560x1440P @30fps, FHD 1920x1080P @60fps (HDR not supported), FHD 1920x1080P @30fps [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **Wi-Fi band:** Dual-Band WiFi 6: 5GHz (default) and 2.4GHz [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **GPS:** Built-in Quad-Mode GPS: GPS (US), Galileo (EU), Beidou (CN), GLONASS (RU) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **Parking mode requirements:** Requires ROVE Ultimate USB Type-C Hardwire Kit (NOT included) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]. Three modes available: 1FPS Time-Lapse + G-Sensor, Collision Detection Only, or Motion Detection & G-Sensor [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **Operating temperature:** 14°F (-10°C) to 131°F (55°C) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

- **LED/indicator meanings:** Blinking RED LED = Recording video; Solid RED LED = Not recording video; NO RED LED = Camera turned OFF [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

## How-to procedures

### Power on/off

**Auto Power ON:** Camera starts automatically when receives power (e.g., car engine starts) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Auto Power OFF:** Camera stops recording, saves last video, and turns OFF automatically 1-2 seconds after power disconnects [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Manual Power ON:** Press Power button once [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Manual Power OFF:** Press and hold Power button for 2-3 seconds [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

### Format the memory card

**Before formatting:** Backup any wanted videos — **DESTRUCTIVE: All data will be deleted** [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

1. Press OK button to stop video recording first or clear any error message on screen [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
2. Press M (Menu) button once to enter Video Settings [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
3. Press M button again to switch to System Settings [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
4. Press D (Down) button to scroll down until you see "FORMAT" [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
5. Press OK button to enter Format submenu [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
6. Press D button to highlight "FORMAT" [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
7. Press OK button to confirm formatting [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Recommended:** Format every 3 months for smooth operation [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

### Parking mode

**Prerequisites:** ROVE Ultimate USB Type-C Hardwire Kit required (NOT included) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Three parking mode options:**

1. **Time-Lapse 1fps + G-Sensor** (records continuously, 1 frame per second; locks 1-min video if G-Sensor triggered) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

2. **Collision Detection Only** (camera OFF, wakes on impact, records 1-min, locks, turns OFF again) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

3. **Motion Detection + G-Sensor** (camera idle with LCD off, records when motion detected; G-Sensor triggers 1-min locked recording) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**How it works (general):**
- When car turns OFF, camera automatically enters parking mode after 1-2 seconds [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
- When car starts next time, voice alert states "Parking Mode got activated while you were away" if event occurred [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
- GPS and WiFi automatically turn OFF during parking mode to save power [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Cleaning temperature safety:** Camera has built-in temperature sensor; will automatically shut down in extreme heat to prevent damage [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

### G-sensor

**Recording G-Sensor (while driving):**
- Automatically locks current video file when impact detected [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
- Level adjustable in Video Settings > Video Recording G-Sensor [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Parking Mode G-Sensor:**
- Levels 1-9: 1 = lowest impact detection (most sensitive), 9 = highest impact detection (least sensitive) [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
- Default recommendation: Find balance where closing car door doesn't trigger false alert [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

### Loop recording

**To enable/configure:**
1. Go to Video Settings > Loop Recording [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
2. Options: OFF, 1-MIN (default), 3-MIN, 5-MIN, 10-MIN [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**How it works:** When normal + event videos exceed 90% of card capacity, oldest unlocked files are deleted automatically [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
- Normal videos: 60% allocation
- Event (locked) videos: 30% allocation — when full, oldest locked videos also deleted [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**OFF setting:** Records 4GB files until card full, then stops with "Card Full" message — will NOT overwrite [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

### CPL filter installation

1. **Power OFF dash cam first** [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
2. Clean lens with microfiber cloth [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
3. Remove protective film from both sides of CPL filter [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

5. Place white paper on dashboard under camera to create reflection [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
6. Rotate front part of filter while observing viewfinder until glare from white paper disappears [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
7. Test in various lighting conditions [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Note:** Remove CPL filter for night driving — reduces available light, darkens footage [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

## Troubleshooting playbook

### Wi-Fi: phone joins camera Wi-Fi then drops / "no internet"

**Likely causes (most likely first):**
1. Normal behavior — camera WiFi does NOT provide internet, only local connection [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
2. Android phone auto-disconnecting due to "No Internet" detection [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**Questions/checks:**
- Is this an Android phone? (iPhone shows "No Internet" but doesn't drop)
- Did the "No Internet" / "Stay Connected?" pop-up appear?

**Fixes:**
1. **Android:** Wait for pop-up notification (10-15 seconds), tap "Always Connect" or "Don't ask again" then "YES" [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
2. **iPhone:** Ignore "No Internet Connection" message — this is expected [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]
3. Ensure staying within 10M range with no obstructions [manual: ROVE-R2-4K-DUAL-PRO]

**When to escalate:** If connection still drops after confirming correct pop-up response — hardware WiFi module issue
