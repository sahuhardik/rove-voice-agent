# ROVE R2-4K (original, mini-USB power) — knowledge pack

## How to recognise this model

**Physical cues:**
- 2.4" LCD screen [manual: dc3831]
- Mini-USB power input / data output slot (not USB-C) — located on the bottom right side of the camera [manual: dc3831]
- 150° A+ Ultra HD Wide Angle lens [manual: dc3831]
- Physical buttons: Power, Menu/Mode, Up, Emergency Lock/WiFi, Down, OK/Confirm — **not touch screen** [manual: dc3831]
- Reset button on the bottom of the unit [manual: dc3831]

**On-screen:**
- Model shows as "R2-4K" or "ROVE R2-4K" [manual: dc3831]

**Wi-Fi network name pattern:**
- WiFi SSID: "ROVE R2-4K-********" (asterisks represent unique ID) [manual: dc3831]
- Default password: 12345678 [manual: dc3831]

**How it differs from other Rove models:**
- Uses "ROVE" app only (not other Rove apps) — page 36 states: "ROVE R2-4K model uses 'ROVE' App only" [manual: dc3831]
- Mini-USB connector, not USB-C [manual: dc3831]

---

## Key facts

**Power and battery/supercapacitor:**
- Built-in Li-ion battery 250mAh — **NOT a supercapacitor** [manual: dc3831]
- Internal battery lasts only 2–5 minutes depending on video resolution (2160P @24fps: 2–3 min; 1440P @30fps: 3–5 min; 720P @30fps: 7–8 min) [manual: dc3831]
- Battery purpose: save last video safely, power off when car turned off, and enable Parking Mode [manual: dc3831]

**Memory card requirements:**
- Type: Micro-SD (TF Card) [manual: dc3831]
- Speed: Class 10, U3 or higher required for 4K video [manual: dc3831]
- Capacity: Up to 512GB max [manual: dc3831]
- Recommended brand: SAMSUNG Class 10, U3 [manual: dc3831]
- **Warning against Sandisk and third-party sellers (eBay, etc.) due to fake cards** [manual: dc3831]

**Video resolutions available:**
- 2880x2160P @24fps (4K)
- 2560x1440P @30fps
- 2304x1296P @30fps
- 1920x1080P @60fps (Full HD)
- 1920x1080P @30fps
- 1280x720P @120fps (slow-motion capable)
- 1280x720P @60fps
- 1280x720P @30fps [manual: dc3831]

**Wi-Fi:**
- Built-in WiFi, single band (no 5G mentioned) [manual: dc3831]
- Range: approximately 10 feet with no obstructions [manual: dc3831]

**GPS:**
- Built-in GPS for Geo-Tagging [manual: dc3831]
- GPS coordinates recorded in latitude/longitude [manual: dc3831]
- "Live Speed" display on LCD optional [manual: dc3831]

**Parking mode requirements:**
- Requires hard-wire kit for continuous power [manual: dc3831]
- Uses internal battery for 1-minute recording when triggered by G-sensor vibration [manual: dc3831]

**Operating temperature:**
- -22°F (-30°C) to 176°F (80°C) [manual: dc3831]
- Working humidity: 10–90% [manual: dc3831]

**LED meanings:**
- **Red LED blinking** = Recording video
- **Red LED solid** = Not recording video
- **No red LED** = Camera turned off
- **Green LED solid** = Internal battery charging
- **No green LED** = Internal battery fully charged [manual: dc3831]

## How-to procedures

### Power on/off

**Auto power on:** Camera turns on and starts recording automatically when it receives power (car engine started) [manual: dc3831]

**Auto power off:** Saves last video file and powers off automatically within 4–5 seconds when power disconnected [manual: dc3831]

**Manual power on:** Press power button once [manual: dc3831]

**Manual power off:** Press and hold power button for 2–3 seconds [manual: dc3831]

**Note:** If car's 12V outlet is always HOT (constant power), auto ON/OFF won't work — requires fuse box wiring change or hard-wire kit [manual: dc3831]

---

### Format the card

**Prerequisite:** Stop recording first by pressing OK if recording [manual: dc3831]

1. Press **M button once** to enter video settings [manual: dc3831]
2. Press **M button again** to switch to System Settings [manual: dc3831]
3. Press **Down button** to scroll to **FORMAT** [manual: dc3831]
4. Press **OK** to enter format submenu [manual: dc3831]
5. Press **Down button** to highlight **FORMAT** [manual: dc3831]
6. Press **OK** to confirm [manual: dc3831]
7. Press **M button** to exit settings [manual: dc3831]

**DESTRUCTIVE** — All data on memory card will be deleted. Recommend backup first. [manual: dc3831]

---

### Reset to defaults (software reset)

1. Go to **System Settings** > scroll to **Default Setting** [manual: dc3831]
2. Select **OK** to reset all settings to factory defaults [manual: dc3831]

**DESTRUCTIVE** — All settings reset to factory defaults. [manual: dc3831]

**Hardware reset (if frozen):** Press **RESET button** on bottom of camera **once** with paper clip or pen tip while camera is ON — restarts camera without deleting settings [manual: dc3831]

---

### Set date/time and time zone

**Method 1 — Manual:**
1. Go to **System Settings** > **Date/Time** [manual: dc3831]
2. Select date and time manually [manual: dc3831]

**Method 2 — GPS/Time Zone (recommended):**
1. Go to **System Settings** > **Time Zone Setting** [manual: dc3831]
2. Select correct **UTC/GMT value** for your location [manual: dc3831]
3. Press **OK** to confirm [manual: dc3831]
4. Camera will set date/time automatically when GPS signal locks [manual: dc3831]

**USA GMT values (standard time Nov–March):**
- Hawaii: GMT (-10:00)
- Alaska: GMT (-9:00)
- Pacific: GMT (-8:00) [default]
- Mountain: GMT (-7:00)
- Central: GMT (-6:00)
- Eastern: GMT (-5:00) [manual: dc3831]

**USA GMT values:** use the daylight-savings column when it applies (e.g., Pacific is GMT (-8:00), and GMT (-7:00) during daylight savings) [manual: dc3831]

---

### Connect to phone app over Wi-Fi

**Before starting:** Download "ROVE" app from App Store or Google Play Store [manual: dc3831]

1. **From camera:** Long press **Emergency Lock button** for **2 seconds** to turn WiFi On [manual: dc3831]
2. **From phone:** Go to phone Settings > WiFi > select **ROVE R2-4K-********** network [manual: dc3831]
3. Enter password: **12345678** [manual: dc3831]
4. **Android extra step:** When "No Internet" notification appears, tap it, check "Don't ask again for this network," then tap **YES** to stay connected [manual: dc3831]
5. Open **ROVE** app — camera will sync automatically, live video feed should appear [manual: dc3831]

**Disconnect WiFi:** Long press **Emergency Lock button** for **2 seconds** to return to video mode [manual: dc3831]

---

### Download/playback footage

**On camera (Playback Mode):**
1. Press **M button** for **2 seconds** to switch modes (repeat to reach Playback Mode) [manual: dc3831]
2. Use **Up/Down buttons** to scroll files [manual: dc3831]
3. Press **OK** to play/pause [manual: dc3831]
4. During playback: **Up button** = reverse fast forward; **Down button** = fast forward [manual: dc3831]

**On PC/MAC:**
- Remove memory card and insert directly, OR
- Connect with included **2.5FT USB Data Cable**, select **"Connect as USB Flash Drive"** when prompted [manual: dc3831]
- Find **NOVATEK** folder for unlocked videos, **RO** folder for locked videos [manual: dc3831]
- **Recommended player:** VLC Media Player (free at www.videolan.org) [manual: dc3831]
- **Do NOT use Windows Media Player** — not made for 4K videos [manual: dc3831]

**On smartphone via WiFi:**
- Connect via WiFi procedure above, use ROVE app to view/download [manual: dc3831]

---

### Lock/protect a file

**During recording:** Press **Emergency Lock button** once to lock current loop cycle video file — prevents deletion by loop recording [manual: dc3831]

**In Playback Mode:**
1. Enter Playback Mode, navigate to file [manual: dc3831]
2. Go to **Playback Settings** > **Protect** > **Lock Current** [manual: dc3831]

**Lock all files:** Playback Settings > Protect > **Lock All** [manual: dc3831]

---

### Parking mode

**Purpose:** Records for 1 minute and locks video when vibration detected while parked [manual: dc3831]

**To enable:**
1. Go to **Video Settings** > **Parking Mode** [manual: dc3831]
2. Select **ON** [manual: dc3831]

**Alternative shortcut:** Long press **Down button** for **2 seconds** to toggle Parking Mode ON/OFF [manual: dc3831]

**Requirements:** Hard-wire kit for continuous power (sold separately) [manual: dc3831]

**Testing procedure:** Turn ON Parking Mode, set G-Sensor to Low/Medium, turn OFF camera (hold power 2 sec), shake camera — should turn ON for 1 min then OFF [manual: dc3831]

**IMPORTANT:** Keep Parking Mode OFF when not installed in car, otherwise camera will randomly turn on/off from movement [manual: dc3831]

---

### Motion detection

**Purpose:** Records only when motion detected in front of camera — for surveillance when parked [manual: dc3831]

**To enable:**
1. Go to **Video Settings** > **Motion Detection** [manual: dc3831]
2. Select **ON** [manual: dc3831]

**Alternative shortcut:** Long press **Up button** for **2 seconds** to toggle Motion Detection ON/OFF [manual: dc3831]

**CAUTION:** NOT recommended while driving — will cause missing video seconds. Designed for parked use only. Requires hard-wire kit for standby power. [manual: dc3831]

---

### G-sensor

**Purpose:** Detects impact/gravitational forces to automatically lock video files [manual: dc3831]

**Settings:**
- **0** = OFF
- **1** = Low Impact Detection
- **5** = Medium Impact Detection (Default)
- **9** = High Impact Detection [manual: dc3831]

**To change:** Video Settings > **Gsensor** [manual: dc3831]

**Note:** Also affects Parking Mode sensitivity [manual: dc3831]

---

### Loop recording

**Settings:** Video Settings > **Loop Recording** [manual: dc3831]

**Options:**
- **OFF** — 4GB max files, "Card Full" message when full, stops recording
- **1-MIN**
- **3-MIN** (Default)
- **5-MIN**
- **10-MIN** [manual: dc3831]

---

**During recording:** Press **Up button** once to mute/unmute microphone [manual: dc3831]

**In settings:** Video Settings > **Record Audio** — **ON** (default) or **OFF** [manual: dc3831]

**Likely causes:**
1. Android phone auto-disconnecting due to "no internet" detection [manual: dc3831]
2. Being out of 10-foot range [manual: dc3831]

## Safety and data-loss warnings

**Heat exposure:**
- Do not expose to temperatures above 140°F (60°C) or leave in direct sunlight [manual: dc3831]
- Battery can explode or cause fire above 176°F/80°C [manual: dc3831]
- Remove from vehicle when parked to prevent theft and heat damage [manual: dc3831]

**Power safety:**
- Use only supplied charger or phone USB charger under 3.1 AMP [manual: dc3831]
- Do NOT connect to PC for charging [manual: dc3831]
- Do not use damaged charger cords [manual: dc3831]

**Mounting safety:**
- Do not block driver's view or airbag deployment zones [manual: dc3831]
- Do not place loose on dashboard [manual: dc3831]
- Be careful with side curtain airbags when routing cables [manual: dc3831]

**Driving safety:**
- Do not operate or change settings while driving — pull over first [manual: dc3831]

**Memory card data loss:**
- **DESTRUCTIVE** — Formatting erases all data [manual: dc3831]
- **DESTRUCTIVE** — Delete All erases all videos and photos except protected files [manual: dc3831]
- Do not remove card while saving or reading data [manual: dc3831]
- Company NOT responsible for data loss under any circumstances [manual: dc3831]

**After accident:**
- Retrieve footage before using camera again — minor impacts may not trigger G-sensor lock [manual: dc3831]
