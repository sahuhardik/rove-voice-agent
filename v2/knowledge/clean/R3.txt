Troubleshooting Guide for ROVE R3 Dash Cam: If none of the troubleshooting steps work, please email customer care
for assistance with a manual firmware update. Camera Installation and Power Supply: Ensure that the camera is securely locked in place (Page 84). If you're using a non-ROVE USB Car Charger, your car's direct USB might not provide enough power, which can cause reboots (Page 85). Recording Issues: There are three possible reasons why
recording may start and stop even with properly connected cables: 1. Using a low-quality memory card. 2. Rear camera connector not connected properly. Display and Touch Screen Issues: If icons are not visible or the touch screen is unresponsive after updating firmware, it's likely due to a failed update process (Page 86). Rear Camera Live View
Issue: There could be three reasons why rear camera live feed is not showing on the main screen: 1. Rear camera cable connected to sticky mount power port. 2. Rear camera not securely connected. Wi-Fi Connection Issues: Apple devices may disconnect and reconnect to your car's Wi-Fi due to priority switching (Page 88). Reconnecting to ROVE
App: Uninstall and reinstall the app, then sign in and reconnect to R3's WiFi connection (Page 89). G-Sensor Settings: Update firmware to fix minor bug with compass direction. Set Parking Mode G-sensor feature accordingly to avoid false positive alarms. Firmware Update Issue: If your camera has an old firmware version, update it remotely using
